## 2. World & Lore Setting

### The Backstory
Following an abrupt and chaotic wave of hostile corporate acquisitions ("Sheller Corp and their hostile takeover permits"), human workers and miners are left stranded across the lunar surface. With central command collapsed and corporate enforcers closing in, survivors must mine regolith, forge makeshift habitats, build automated defensive grids, and form fragile alliances to maintain control of lunar territories.

### World Atmosphere & Tone
* **Dark Corporate Satire:** A blend of bleak industrial dystopia and tongue-in-check corporate sci-fi humor.
* **Key Characters:**
  * **Eggie Laang:** A retired, vape-smoking veteran miner stationed at the Armstrong Dome. Eggie acts as an initial point of contact for new arrivals, offering guidance, basic lore, and starter supply crates.
